# Privacy

TEMPLATE: review before publishing and update it before any release that adds ads or analytics.

**What OmniFerry does with your data**
- Files and clipboard content go directly from one of your devices to another over your local network, encrypted with TLS.
  They are never uploaded to a server operated by the author.
- The app stores on your device: settings, a list of paired devices, its own certificate and key, unfinished transfers, and a log
  file. Nothing is sent anywhere automatically.
- While running, the app announces its name, OS and certificate fingerprint to the local network (mDNS) so your devices can find it.
  Anyone on the same network can see this.
- The browser gateway, when enabled, is reachable by anyone on your network who has its link.

**Accounts, analytics, ads**
- This version has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.
- If a future version adds ads or analytics, this document will be updated first and the release notes will say so.
